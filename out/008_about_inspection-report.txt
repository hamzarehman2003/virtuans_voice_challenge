Recognition By BSO and KHDA British Schools Overseas Inspection Report 2023 – Rated “OUTSTANDING” Sunmarke was rated ‘ Outstanding ’ by the British Schools Overseas (BSO) inspection report of 2023. The BSO is an inspection agency that follows the framework and standards of the Department of Education (DfE) in the UK for National Curriculum schools. Click here to view our inspection report. KHDA Inspection Report 2023-24 – Rated “Very Good” Sunmarke achieved “ Outstanding ” and “ Very Good ” ratings in the Knowledge and Human Development Agency (KHDA) and Dubai School Inspection Bureau (DSIB) Report (2023-2024) and received an overall “ Very Good ”. Click here to download the full report. 5 OUTSTANDING features the inspectors found in EVERY phase of Sunmarke School: Health and safety, including arrangements for child protection and safeguarding Care and support Students’ personal development Social responsibility and innovation skills Understanding of Islamic values and awareness of Emirati and world cultures 10 AMAZING things the inspectors say about Sunmarke School: We are highly inclusive. We have a clear vision for ensuring the wellbeing of everyone, which is founded on Positive Education. We use rigorous policies, systems, and practices that ensure students’ safety. We demonstrate mutual respect and open communication that contribute to a thriving leadership culture. We have premises which are of excellent quality and are meticulously maintained. We have outstanding resources to support teaching and learning. We use assessment well in lesson planning and gauge understanding through probing questioning. Sunmarke students demonstrate a strong sense of responsibility and independence; they interact with maturity and confidence. Sunmarke students are enthusiastic learners who have a well-developed sense of conservation and sustainability.
