Login Apply Visit Enquire VLE Login Enriched Learning Learning > Primary > Enriched Learning Enriched Learning Extra-Curricular Activities to Expand Horizons beyond the Timetabled Curriculum These school-run activities conducted by our academic staff vary from term to term and year to year depending on children’s choice and staffing. Clubs are held from Monday to Thursday after school. A typical range of clubs and activities includes: Year 2 Fine Motor Skills Fine motor club is an opportunity to develop your fine motor skills and communication skills by working with partners. Students will take part in a range of creative activities which support in fine motor development and handwriting skills. Mathletics Club An amazing opportunity for students to develop a lifelong love of maths through: Confidence with maths facts fluency Enjoyment of solving maths problems Tackling maths puzzles/games Fun with maths song and play Yoga & Mindfulness At Sunmarke, well-being and positivity are at the heart of everything we do. In this yoga and mindfulness club, students get the chance to end the day calmly with some relaxing activities that promote mindfulness, well-being and appreciation. Multi Sports Want to get active and have some fun? This club allows children to enjoy energetic activities while being active and blowing off some steam. Students will focus on developing basic movement skills through fun games while coming together to make new friends and work in teams. Fun with French This club promotes the love of language and the amazing experiences that come along with learning. Students will get the chance to become more confident in French and learn new vocabulary through games and engaging activities. Board Games Strategy and focus are major skills of almost every board game. In this activity, students have the chance to develop these skills in an interactive group setting. Have a favourite board game or one that you think people wouldn’t have played before? Bring it along to Board Games Club and enjoy developing your critical thinking, problem-solving and strategic skills. Photography Club If you would like to develop your photography and creative outlook, this is an excellent activity for you. During this club, you will learn about what good photography requires and how to edit and improve your images. Come explore the artist within! Art Club Art club gives students the chance to learn and develop art skills, using drawing and sketching techniques you will learn to create inventive and creative artwork. Work alongside other talented students to enhance your creative flair and enjoy learning new things. Year 3 & Year 4 Book Worms In Book Worms, students will be able to develop an inner love of reading. In this club, you will read both individually and as a group, writing book reviews and promoting a love of reading across the school. Creative Writing Club “Writing is the painting of the voice.” Get your favourite pens ready as we embark on an adventure where your creative dreams can come true. Creative writing club gives all students the chance to develop new vocabulary and enhance their love of writing. Fun and engaging topics ensure students get a chance to develop their own stories. Digital Art Are you an aspiring artist or graphic designer? Join the digital art club to develop your digital art skills. You will work collaboratively to grow your understanding of digital artwork using the Sketchbook art application. Year 3 and Year 4 Choir Join the Year 3 and Year 4 choir to develop your inner performer. You will have the chance to enhance your creative talents and grow in confidence as you practise a range of songs throughout the term. This is a great chance to start your stage career early. Year 3 and Year 4 Yoga & Mindfulness At Sunmarke, well-being and positivity are at the heart of everything we do. In this yoga and mindfulness club, students get the chance to end the day calmly with some relaxing activities that promote mindfulness, well-being and appreciation. Creators club Do you enjoy being creative? If so, come along to Creators Club where you will have the opportunity to build your self-esteem, resilience, and gain a sense of accomplishment whilst engaging in fun creative arts and crafts projects. Maths Stars Join the Maths Stars to explore maths in a fun and creative way, whilst also showing off your artistic skills! Each week, you will use your maths knowledge to create a piece of colourful and interesting artwork. You will also have the opportunity to explore some challenging maths problems and investigations. Poetry Club With a mix of creative writing skills and performance poetry, this club is perfect to build your confidence in both writing and public speaking. Zumba Dance Get fit and active with the Zumba club; you will have had fun dancing and developing your rhythm. An extremely fun and engaging way for students to express themselves and stay active. Woodwork Join the woodwork club to gain lots of new key skills. In this club, you will develop your design and technology skills, creating fun and exciting woodwork projects. Islamic Culture Looking for a fun and educational extracurricular activity? Join our Islamic culture club! This club is open to all students who are interested in learning more about the rich history and traditions of Islam. Through engaging activities and discussions, we explore various aspects of Islamic culture, including art, music, literature, cuisine, and more. Whether you are a practising Muslim or simply interested in learning more about this fascinating culture, our Islamic culture club is the perfect place for you! Arabic Reading Are you looking to improve your Arabic reading skills? Look no further than our Arabic Reading Club! Our club is open to students of all levels who are interested in enhancing their reading proficiency in Arabic. Through guided reading sessions, group discussions and interactive activities, you’ll have the opportunity to build your vocabulary, improve your pronunciation, and develop your comprehension skills. Our club members come from diverse backgrounds and share a passion for learning the Arabic language and its rich literary tradition. Join us and expand your knowledge of this beautiful language while making new friends and having fun! Arabic Arts and Crafts Join our Arabic arts and crafts club and explore the beauty of Arabic calligraphy, textile art and more! Our club is open to students of all skill levels who are interested in creating unique and beautiful works of art inspired by the rich cultural heritage of the Arabic-speaking world. Our experienced instructors will guide you through the process of creating your own masterpieces, using a variety of materials and techniques. You’ll have the opportunity to learn about the history and symbolism behind Arabic art and craft traditions, while developing your own creativity and imagination. Our club is a welcoming and inclusive community where you can express yourself, make new friends, and discover the joy of creating something beautiful with your own hands. Join us today and let your creativity soar! Emirati Culture club Are you fascinated by Emirati culture? Do you want to learn more about the traditions and customs of the United Arab Emirates? Look no further than our Emirati culture club! Our club is open to all students who are interested in exploring the unique and diverse culture of the UAE. Through engaging activities, discussions and events, we delve into various aspects of Emirati culture, including art, music, cuisine and more. You’ll have the opportunity to connect with other students who share your interests, and learn from knowledgeable guest speakers and experts in the field.
