Sensibilities Our classes in music, art, computing, languages, PE and others are carefully planned to match each year group’s STEAM projects wherever possible. They also embrace our Positive Education ethos and make meaningful links with moral, social and cultural studies objectives. Students are taught to appreciate art and design from different periods, created by famous composers, engineers, linguists and sporting heroes. This enhances their knowledge of form, texture, pitch, tone, coding and robotics, pronunciation and composition, and timing and precision. These skills are further built upon in ECAs, including our Performing Arts elective that prepares students for the annual musical performance. Stimulating displays in classrooms and corridors result in widespread appreciation of their achievements and efforts throughout the year. Music There is something for everyone in Sunmarke’s music department. Our specialist music teachers ignite every child’s passion for music, be they a grade 7 pianist or an enthusiast in different musical genres. Students are encouraged to perform as much as possible: in assemblies, concerts, school productions – our school is their stage! See the highlight pictures below from our previous musical, which brought High School Musical to life with our talented Primary students. Stay tuned for this year’s spectacular production of Roald Dahl’s Matilda the Musical ! Performances There are many opportunities for students to shine on stage at Sunmarke Primary, with a vibrant calendar of performances that celebrate creativity, confidence and collaboration. Each year, our students captivate audiences through a wide range of events: Winter Performances: Our Year 1 and 2 students take to the stage to share a heart-warming mix of songs, poems and dances, reflecting their learning journeys from Term 1. Snowmarke: A much-anticipated concert-style showcase, Snowmarke features selected students from Years 3 to 6 performing music, dance and drama with exceptional flair. Summer Showcases: Year 3 4 and 5 students take audiences on a musical journey inspired by their curriculum, with original performances that highlight their creativity and enthusiasm. Primary Musical: A highlight of the year, our annual musical sees Year 5 and 6 students audition, rehearse and perform in a full-scale production.
