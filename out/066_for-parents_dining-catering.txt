Enquire VLE Login Apply Visit Enquire VLE Login Food For Parents > Food Dining & Catering Healthy, Wholesome And Nutritious Meals For Your Child We understand the important role that health and nutrition play in a student’s overall wellbeing and therefore, encourage healthy eating habits from an early age. Towards this, we have partnered with Mastercook the catering experts, to tailor-make our dining menus and meal programmes. We have a bright and spacious cafeteria where students can enjoy healthy food options, socialise, and deepen their friendships. They can opt for pre-packaged food in environment-friendly packs as well. We also provide Grab & Go snacks for them. Menu and Pricing: The Mastercook menu offers a selection of nutritious, additive-free and preservative-free school meals. With options from various cuisines, the lunch menu is priced at AED 24.25 Keeping dietary restrictions in mind, the nut-free menu has been crafted with care and quality. Parents of EYFS and Primary students can easily place their orders via the provided link, with lunch delivered directly to their child’s classroom. http://form.jotform.com/Mastercook/Sunmarke-stripe Secondary students can savor their lunch in the school canteen. Payments can be made using credit cards, Apple Pay or Google Pay Health & Safety Mastercook implements robust safety measures in their kitchens, including the use of PPE kits and a Safety Ambassador to oversee quality and hygiene.
