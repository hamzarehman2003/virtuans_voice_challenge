Accessible To All Inclusivity is enshrined in Sunmarke’s ethos where we welcome a diverse range of learners from EYFS to Secondary. The Achievement Centre takes the lead in providing an inclusive education to students in close collaboration with parents, teachers and external specialists. “ Jayne West Head Of Inclusion Laila is a Global Ambassador representing sports for People of Determination worldwide. She was an outstanding member of the Secondary Life Skills Class, where she gained the qualifications to be accepted into Dubai College of Leisure and Tourism further education course. We are all very proud of her as she continues to inspire our students to believe in the dream!!! To facilitate the successful participation and progress of all learners, a range of additional provisions, subject to staffing and availability, are offered at no extra cost. They include, but are not limited to: Interventions for literacy Interventions for mathematics Speaking and listening Social skills Motor skills Directed studies Extra-curricular activities specific to students with SEND (Special Educational Needs & Disabilities) Commitment to Inclusion Our school is also an accredited centre for the BTEC Introductory Awards offering a wide range of both academic and vocational units including Business Administration, Media, Health and Fitness, Leisure and Hospitality. It has a highly successful SEND department which has been commended in our most recent DSIB (Dubai School Inspection Bureau) inspection. We have developed a reliable network of external providers, who parents can contract to provide specialist assessment and therapy services at Sunmarke. These include, but are not limited to: Comprehensive developmental and diagnostic assessment Psycho-educational assessment Behavioural assessment and intervention Occupational Therapy and Sensory Integration assessment and intervention Speech and Language Therapy assessment and intervention Personalised support from a 1:1 Learning Support Assistant Fortes English Language Programme The Fortes English Language Programme is an intensive programme of cross curricular, full immersion pedagogy, with a focus on academic English, alongside every day language and culture. Students learn language alongside new skills and all within a broad range of subjects and activities such as Art, Drama, PE and Sport, Music in addition to the usual core subjects. Students experience accelerated language acquisition and achieve a deeper understanding of subject-specific English across the curriculum.
