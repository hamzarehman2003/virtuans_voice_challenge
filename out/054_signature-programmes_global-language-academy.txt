Global Citizenship Programme Overview Sunmarke’s Global Languages Programme immerses students in two of the world’s most influential languages — Hindi and Mandarin — helping them become confident communicators and empathetic global citizens. Through storytelling, culture, cuisine, arts, and literature, students don’t just learn a language — they experience a civilisation. Who It’s For All students from EYFS to Year 13 — including beginners, heritage learners, and those learning as a foreign language. What Students Experience Hindi Programme: Offered from EYFS to Year 6, focusing on festivals, food, theatre, and personal identity Mandarin Programme: Offered from EYFS to Year 9, aligned with the Youth Chinese Test (YCT) and GCSE Integration of language with culture through activities like calligraphy, traditional dance, tai chi, cooking, and music Celebration assemblies, festivals, and school-wide events for Diwali, Holi, and Chinese New Year Learning Journey EYFS: Gentle immersion through storytelling, songs, and sensory activities Primary: Games, roleplay, cultural crafts, cooking, oral fluency Secondary: Grammar, writing, debating, cross-cultural discussions, YCT and GCSE prep Cultural Fluency Development Guest workshops, performances, and cultural exchanges Integrated storytelling and global themes across languages and literature Personal identity development and intercultural understanding Facilities & Learning Tools Immersive classrooms with cultural artefacts Professional audio-visual tools and gamified apps Culturally responsive library collections in Hindi and Mandarin Curriculum and Assessment Aligned with international language learning pathways Integrated into core curriculum, supported by enrichment and ECAs Assessment includes formative feedback, portfolios , and recognised external qualifications Links to IB and A-Level language options , international university language entry requirements Next Steps Speak to the Head of Global, visit a class, attend a cultural showcase, or explore our Languages programme to begin your child’s multilingual journey.
