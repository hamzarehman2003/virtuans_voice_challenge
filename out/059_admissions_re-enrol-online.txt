an international school offering the best British curriculum education in Dubai to the constant support and confidence families have reposed in us. We encourage students to aim for the stars but we equally hold ourselves to high standards, committed to offering the best academic provision and value-for-money education in the UAE and larger MENA region. To continue your journey with us and to secure a seat for the academic year 2024-25, we encourage all our families to re-enrol online. A non-refundable re-enrolment fee of 5% must be paid by April 30, 2024, and will be deducted from the tuition fee due for the term of joining. Please note that if the re-enrolment fee is not paid by April 30, 2024, the entire Term 1 fee will be invoiced (subject to availability). To re-enrol your child, please click the below link and log on to the VLE portal on your desktop or VLE Mobile App, and complete the Re-enrolment process online.
