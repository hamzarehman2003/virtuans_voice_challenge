Where Each Day Brings A New Learning Adventure Part of the award-winning group of Fortes Education Schools and located within Sunmarke’s campus, Jumeirah International Nurseries (JINS) enables a smooth and seamless transition into the Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) at Sunmarke School. With over 40 years of Early Years excellence, JINS Early Childhood Centre provides an inspiring British education that nurtures the hearts and minds of children aged 45+ days old and sparks infinite potential in all our little learners. Nursery School in Dubai Areas of Learning JINS’ unique educational framework blends the World-Class British EYFS Curriculum with Reggio-Emilia, The Curiosity Approach and Montessori principles to bring hands-on learning experiences to life. Our curriculum is further enriched with STEAM and Positive Education to set the stage for an exciting journey of discovery. Our Early Years Experts create authentic and meaningful learning experiences that embrace the individuality of each child, allowing them to progress and flourish based on their unique interests, abilities and aptitude. These seven areas of learning are essential for your child’s healthy development and form the core framework of our educational programmes: 3 Core Areas of Learning Physical Development Communication and Language Personal, Social and Emotional Development 4 Specific Areas of Learning Literacy Mathematics Understanding of the World Expressive Arts and Design Extraordinary Indoor & Outdoor Spaces With 9 vibrant branches in Dubai, JINS provides rich learning environments that ignite children’s imagination and stimulate endless moments of discovery and exploration. Our nature-inspired Early Childhood Centre in the heart of the JVT community offers stimulating and engaging spaces where every detail is crafted to inspire and nurture young minds. Children at JINS Sunmarke, JVT, have access to large, Reggio-inspired classrooms and a shared courtyard to which all classroom doors open, making it a welcoming space where children explore through sensory and physical play whilst also forging a strong connection with nature through our outdoor mud kitchen and garden areas. Learning Through Play Our passionate educators create joy-filled learning experiences where children ‘learn through play’ and hands-on activities that enhance their understanding and comprehension of the world around them. Our open-ended Reggio-inspired learning resources encourage creativity and problem-solving and enable our children to become confident and independent little learners. Outstanding Care The wellbeing of our children and families is at the heart of what we do. We’re committed to exceeding and maintaining the highest international standards of quality and safety in early childhood education. We are proud holders of the ISO 9001 Certification (Quality Systems) and are the only Nursery in the UAE with the ISO 21001 (Education Systems) Certification. At JINS, we follow the below rigorous health and safety procedures at our Early Childhood Centres: Clean, fresh, and sanitised learning environments Child-friendly changing facilities and toilets located within the classroom Inculcating children with practices of good hygiene throughout the day A full-time nurse on site Doctor visits twice a week Stringent security measures with identifiable lanyards for all adults (parents/visitors/staff) to wear at all times Security gates surrounding the nursery externally and internally All families are invited for a staggered entry week allowing a smooth and steady start for children Transition to Big School At JINS JVT, we help our children develop and secure skills which will be essential for a smooth transition into EYFS at Sunmarke School at the age of 3. We support our young learners as they develop pre-writing and literacy skills and build their knowledge of important mathematical concepts through play and exploration. Children learn how to identify and write their own names and are introduced to the Letters and Sounds and Accelerated Maths Programme. On a day-to-day basis, the children are encouraged to be independent, think critically, ask questions, and use their imagination as they learn and explore together. In addition to this, our children regularly go across and experience the learning spaces in EYFS at Sunmarke School in the Summer Term, allowing them to acclimatise to new environments, practitioners, peers, routines, and levels of expectation. During these ‘Transition Days’ children experience the Forest Schools in EYFS at both schools, visit the libraries and play spaces, and enjoy specialised lessons such as Physical Education and Music.
