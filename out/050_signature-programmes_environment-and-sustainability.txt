Nurturing Tomorrow's Environmental Changemakers Through Forest School Programme Overview Preparing Global Environmental Leaders With climate change at the forefront of global challenges, Sunmarke’s Environment & Sustainability Programme nurtures a deep connection with nature while developing scientific knowledge, eco-leadership, and sustainability values. Our immersive outdoor learning environments — including the Forest School, Zen Garden, Vegetable Garden, and Hydroponic Farming Lab — make sustainability an everyday experience. Who It’s For All students from EYFS to Year 13. The programme is cross-disciplinary and fully integrated into academic and co-curricular learning. Learning Journey EYFS: Sensory play, simple conservation habits, nature appreciation Primary: Ecosystem studies, organic gardening, biodegradable product design Secondary: Environmental research, global climate advocacy, sustainability leadership Natural Learning Environments Forest Garden: Guided biodiversity exploration Vegetable Garden: Organic farming and seasonal harvesting Zen Garden: Mindfulness and reflection in nature Hydroponic Lab: Soil-free, water-conserving agriculture using high-tech systems Signature Programme Elements Climate-focused STEM projects and eco-entrepreneurship Desert, beach, and neighbourhood clean-up drives Biodiversity and conservation field studies Student-led petitions and environmental campaigns Integration of UN Sustainable Development Goals TASS ( The Alliance for Sustainable Schools) – empowering our students to lead sustainable change through global collaboration and impactful green initiatives. Wellbeing Through Nature Stress reduction, focus, creativity, and resilience through outdoor learning Character strengths like empathy, perseverance, and responsibility nurtured in real-world contexts Curriculum Integration Science: Ecology, conservation biology, climate systems Technology: Environmental monitoring, VR tools, climate modelling Engineering: Renewable energy, sustainable design Math: Statistical analysis and modelling of environmental data Languages: Cultural approaches to conservation (Mandarin, Hindi, French) Facilities & Resources Hydroponic stations, composting units, environmental sensors Outdoor classrooms and natural laboratories Community garden and student-led sustainability zones such as Forest School and Zen Garden. Pathways & Progression GCSE, A-Level, BTEC and IB Environmental Systems & Societies Career preparation in sustainability, conservation, and environmental policy Strong university placement record in environmental and science fields Next Steps Join a Forest School session, explore the Hydroponic Lab, or attend our student-led Sustainability Showcase to witness the programme in action.
