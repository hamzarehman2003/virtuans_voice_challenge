Growing up, the Prime Areas help develop skills in 4 Specific Areas: Literacy Mathematics Understanding of the World Expressive Arts and Design Laying the Foundations for Lifelong Learning in FS1 and FS2 At Sunmarke, our Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS), comprising FS1 (Nursery) and FS2 (Reception) , offers a broad, balanced, and enriching curriculum for children aged 3 to 5. Rooted in the British curriculum and recognised as one of Dubai’s leading EYFS providers, our programme nurtures each child’s unique strengths and interests through play-based, inquiry-led learning. A Curriculum Designed Around the Whole Child The EYFS curriculum at FS1 and FS2 is structured around seven key areas of learning and development: Prime Areas (Early Focus): Personal, Social and Emotional Development Physical Development Communication and Language These areas are particularly emphasised in FS1 , helping our youngest learners develop the essential foundations of confidence, relationships, movement, and communication. Specific Areas (Building on the Foundations): Literacy Mathematics Understanding the World Expressive Arts and Design As children progress into FS2 , these specific areas become more prominent, supporting emerging literacy and numeracy skills, creative expression, and a growing awareness of the world. Characteristics of Effective Learning Across FS1 and FS2, we recognise that children learn in different ways. The EYFS framework promotes three key learning characteristics: Playing and Exploring: Investigating and experiencing, ‘having a go’ Active Learning: Concentrating, persevering, and celebrating achievement Creating and Thinking Critically: Developing ideas, making connections, and problem-solving These traits guide how learning is planned and delivered across both FS1 and FS2, ensuring that each child’s development is supported in a way that reflects their interests, needs, and learning styles. Learning Through Play and Projects At Sunmarke, we view play as a powerful vehicle for learning in both FS1 and FS2. Our curriculum is intentionally designed to offer rich, purposeful play experiences that allow children to explore, imagine, and make sense of the world in a way that is meaningful to them. In FS1 , children are encouraged to investigate through hands-on exploration, sensory play, and open-ended experiences that develop early communication, social, and physical skills. As they transition into FS2 , children engage in more structured yet still playful learning that builds early literacy and numeracy skills while continuing to promote creativity and curiosity. Our project-based learning approach further enhances engagement. Each term, both FS1 and FS2 children explore exciting, inquiry-led themes such as “I Believe I Can Fly,” “Somewhere Out There,” and “Up, Up and Away.” These projects spark imagination and are shaped by the children’s own interests and questions, creating a deep sense of ownership and wonder. Whether FS1 children are building a fairy tale castle or FS2 pupils are investigating the planets, our environments are carefully curated to provide challenge, joy, and discovery. Through child-initiated and adult-guided activities, children develop critical thinking, collaboration, and communication—the building blocks of future learning. A Holistic and Nurturing Approach At Sunmarke, we believe that every child in FS1 and FS2 is a unique individual. Our holistic approach to early education goes beyond academics to nurture each child’s emotional, social, physical, and intellectual development. We incorporate globally respected early years methodologies including elements of the Reggio Emilia philosophy , Montessori principles , and the Curiosity Approach . This eclectic framework values child-led exploration, independence, and a strong connection to the learning environment—creating rich opportunities for discovery and expression. Our learning spaces are thoughtfully designed to be calm, beautiful, and engaging, reflecting the importance of the environment as the ‘third teacher’. Teachers and support staff observe closely, respond sensitively, and plan responsively to extend learning in authentic, meaningful ways. Strong partnerships with parents, a passionate team of educators, and a shared belief in the limitless potential of every child create a warm, supportive community. In FS1 and FS2, children feel safe, seen, and inspired—laying a confident foundation for their journey through school and life. Characteristics of Effective Learning The characteristics of Effective Learning describe how children learn and respond to teaching in the Foundation Stage, a factor determined by their individual learning characteristic. Three traits of effective teaching and learning identified by the EYFS are: Playing and Exploring: Finding out, discovering, willing to ‘have a go’ and playing with what they know Active Learning: Being involved and concentrating, trying and persevering, taking pleasure from achievements Creating and Thinking Critically: Developing their own ideas, making links and selecting their own ways to complete tasks 7 Curriculum Areas To enhance the learning experience and make it impactful, Sunmarke has selected 7 curriculum areas taught through unique projects and interests, most of which are child-initiated activities that incorporate elements of imagination, investigation and ‘out of the box’ ideas, concepts and solutions. The EYFS curriculum is designed and based on the philosophy of learning through play which enhances critical thinking abilities, fuels risk-taking and creative skills. Examples of topics are ‘I believe I can fly’, ‘Somewhere out there’ and ‘Up up and away.’ The initiative EYFS curriculum is designed and based on the philosophy of learning through play. Our teaching philosophy is designed to treat all children as unique individuals in their own right. Our holistic approach towards education ensures that parents/carers, class teachers, specialist teachers and learning assistants work very effectively together to support our firm belief that ‘every child is unique’. Our maxim is that happy and well-motivated children are good learners. The Foundation Stage is a safe, secure and friendly environment for our youngest children to gain confidence and independence, as they prepare for the next stage of schooling. It is a foundation for future learning. Characteristics of Effective Learning (CoEL) are a revived element in the current Early Years Foundation Stage Curriculum (EYFS).
