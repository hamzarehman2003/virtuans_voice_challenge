giving our 100% British Values As a British Schools Overseas (BSO) accredited school, Sunmarke School is committed to upholding the values and standards of a world-class British education. This includes a strong focus on the spiritual, moral, social and cultural development of our students, as well as the promotion of respect, tolerance, and responsibility. These principles align seamlessly with the values of the UAE, where respect for diversity and cultural understanding form the foundation of daily life. Together, these shared values shape our school community into one that is inclusive, aspirational, and globally minded. Rule of Law – School policies, codes of conduct, and classroom expectations help pupils understand fairness, responsibility, and the importance of living within agreed rules, reflecting the laws of the UAE. Individual Liberty – We encourage independence and student choice in learning, while ensuring safety and guidance so pupils understand the balance between rights and responsibilities. Mutual Respect – Our Positive Education ethos, house system, and community partnerships promote kindness and respect in daily interactions across the school. Tolerance of Different Faiths and Beliefs – We celebrate the UAE’s cultural and religious diversity, providing opportunities to learn about and respect different traditions while remaining rooted in our own values. Student Voice & Representation – Pupils are given a voice through Student Council, Sports Leaders, and other leadership opportunities, ensuring they understand the importance of fairness, collaboration, and shared decision-making. Why Educate Your Child at Sunmarke? A Rigorous & Ambitious Education We offer rigorous, ambitious and holistic education emphasising academic excellence and participation in diverse extracurricular activities enabling children to achieve outstanding academic, social and personal success in a stimulating and supportive environment. Outstanding Senior Leaders & Teachers All our inspiring senior leaders, teachers and staff are trained in Positive Education and best practices in education, and are provided continuous professional development and training to keep abreast of the latest developments in pedagogy.
