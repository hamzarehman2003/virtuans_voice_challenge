Login Apply Visit Enquire VLE Login Sunmarke Mini MBA Learning > Secondary > Sunmarke Mini MBA Sunmarke Mini MBA A One-Year Programme Delivered In Conjunction With The University Of Birmingham, Dubai The programme follows the format of a traditional adult MBA. Modules are taught by international professors at the University of Birmingham, Dubai, and in school by qualified Business and Economics Teachers. The Sunmarke mini-MBA is structured around practical themes. The real-world focus ensures we develop reflective leaders who can navigate the most challenging global trends and tensions. Throughout the programme students are also invited to lectures by guest speakers and visiting educational professors at the University of Birmingham, Dubai. Modules covered during the programme include: Economics Finance Marketing ‘Marketing to the GCC’ Upon completion of the modules students then undertake a Business Project , which is designed to test their team-work, communication, leadership, negotiation, decision-making and risk-taking skills 1 / 11 Facebook Twitter Pinterest
