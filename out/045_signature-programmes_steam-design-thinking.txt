in Our SPACE-X Innovation Lab Programme Overview At Sunmarke School, the STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, Mathematics) Signature Programme transforms students into inventors, engineers, designers, and problem-solvers through immersive, real-world learning. Anchored in our state-of-the-art SPACE-X Innovation and Design Thinking Lab, the programme encourages experimentation, collaboration, and creativity from EYFS to Year 11. Who It’s For All students from EYFS through Year 11 — regardless of prior technical knowledge. Support is available for all learning needs, including Students of Determination. Learning Journey EYFS & KS1 (Ages 3–7): Discovery-based play, early coding, foundational design skills KS2 (Ages 7–11): Engineering challenges, collaborative builds, STEAM fairs KS3–KS4 (Ages 11–16): Advanced prototyping, mechanical systems, venture incubation, AI programming What Makes It Unique Integration of Stanford Design Thinking methodology Dedicated SPACE-X Lab with 3D printers, robotics, electronics, and fabrication zones Hands-on prototyping, AI, and entrepreneurial ventures Industry mentorship and venture incubators Seamless integration with subjects like English, Business, Maths, and Art Facilities & Resources SPACE-X Innovation Lab with professional-grade tools Design Thinking Labs Digital media and design studios Electronics and robotics workstations Maker spaces and 3D fabrication labs Signature Opportunities Shark Tank-style pitch competitions Real-world product launches through the Venture Incubator Industry expert mentorship and career shadowing STEAM showcases and student exhibitions Student Outcomes Readiness for careers in innovation, tech, engineering, design, and entrepreneurship Pathways to GCSE, A-Level, IB, and BTEC qualifications in relevant fields Enhanced creativity, analytical thinking, and collaboration skills Next Steps Book a lab tour, attend our annual STEAM showcase, or speak to our coordinators to explore how your child can engage with innovation at every stage of their learning.
