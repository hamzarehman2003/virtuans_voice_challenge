Sunmarke’s biggest strength is its passionate senior leadership team and faculty of teachers whose world-class experience in teaching, learning and administration has led the institution to be rated as one of the best schools in Dubai. Our educators are commited to building character, wellbeing and values in a child, along with ensuring academic excellence with a strong belief in Sunmarke’s disruptively innovative approach to education. The school is guided by a Board of Governors comprising teachers, senior leaders, and a Parents’ Body who bring their rich professional experience, insight and advice to building one of the foremost British curriculum schools in Dubai. Sunmarke is further nurtured by a Board of Directors with over four decades of experience in school management. The Board supports the school’s growth by onboarding world-class senior leaders and teachers, and investing in up-to-date educational practices and facilities. Our educators hail from top-ranked universities in the UK. The Director of Fortes Education, Dr Neil Hopkin, the ex-principal at Sunmarke school is a globally renowned Principal and thought leader in education. Our senior leaders are nationally recognised education practitioners in the UK and our teachers have been associated with leading schools in the UK and elsewhere in the world.
