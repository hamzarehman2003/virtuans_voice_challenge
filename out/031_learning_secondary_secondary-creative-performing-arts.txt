Talents In Students Sunmarke is an international school where creative arts are given as equal importance as other subjects. Talented students blessed with imagination and creativity can express themselves through our deeply arts-infused programme in Music, Theatre, the Visual Arts and/or Dance and Media and Communication. The arts-infused programme in music, theatre, the visual arts, dance, and media and communication (where student reporters produce an online newspaper, podcasts, and blogs), develop skills in literacy, presentation, and collaboration. The ‘Sunmarke School Tube’ GCSE and Post-16 Media and Creative Art students have created the Sunmarke School Tube Channel. All videos are filmed and edited by our creative students. Enjoy and subscribe here . Sunmarke Shorts VLOG The Media and Communication focus is interesting as it allows interested students to join Sunmarke’s editorial team where they become student reporters, producing an online and physical newspaper, podcasts and blogs. These fun activities enhance their presentation, collaboration, and communication skills. Sunmarke offers students plenty of opportunities to tap into their creative side. Take a deeper look at our Creative Arts subjects below: Art Our Arts Department allows students to experiment in different disciplines such as fine art, digital art, textiles, sculpting, pottery and print-making. We have some outstanding wall art murals that Primary and Secondary school students collaborate on around the school. Music Sunmarke benefits from the Fortes’ VIP Academy for Performing Arts and a team of professional music teachers who train students in varied musical instruments and skills. All students are encouraged to develop an appreciation of music. Every student learns an instrument of his or her choice and many develop mastery and choose to prepare for the Trinity/ABRSM music exams. Talented students are nurtured to consider music as a choice of higher study, while some choose to just have fun jamming in a band. Also, keep an eye out for the ‘ Lunchtime Pop-Up Series ‘ where our hottest musicians perform around school and delight all with their musical talents. Drama With inspirational drama and music teachers and an Auditorium, Black Box studio and several studios for the Performing Arts, Sunmarke provides students ample opportunities to contribute to various aspects of a theatrical production as performers or crew. Some of our most recent productions include We Will Rock You, Little Shop of Horrors, and The Wiz .
