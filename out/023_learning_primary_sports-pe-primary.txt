Visit Enquire VLE Login Sports & PE Learning > Primary > Sports & PE Sports & PE ‘Teamwork makes the dream work!’ is the motto of our sporting heroes at Sunmarke Primary At Sunmarke School, our world-class PE department offers a diverse range of squads and teams, providing students with ample opportunities to excel in various sports. Whether your child aspires to be a top footballer or a champion swimmer, Sunmarke nurtures their ambitions and helps turn their dreams into reality. Our commitment to sporting excellence is evident in our recent achievements. In March 2024, our primary students clinched first place overall at the prestigious U11 British Schools in the Middle East (BSME) Games. This remarkable feat included victories in girls’ football, athletics, and boys’ basketball, highlighting the dedication and talent of our young athletes. For students who enjoy sports and fitness but prefer a non-competitive environment, we offer a variety of clubs and activities designed to enhance their physical well-being. Our passionate and dedicated PE staff work diligently to build each child’s stamina and resilience, ensuring they perform at their highest level.
